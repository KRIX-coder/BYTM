Who We Are

Built by IP Experts, Trusted by Founders.

BYTM was founded with a simple belief: every business - from a bootstrapped D2C startup to a multinational enterprise - deserves world-class trademark protection without the complexity or inflated costs of traditional law firms.

Over the past 15+ years, our team has filed and prosecuted thousands of trademarks across India and 100+ jurisdictions. We combine deep legal expertise with modern technology - automated search, real-time case tracking, and WhatsApp-first communication - so you always know where your brand stands.
