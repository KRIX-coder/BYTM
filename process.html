Our Process

From Brand Idea to Registered Trademark.

A clear, predictable, 4-step process that takes the guesswork out of trademark registration.

Home/Process
01
Day 1 - Within 24 hrs

Free Trademark Search

Share your brand name, logo, and business category. Our team runs a comprehensive conflict check across the Trademark Registry, common-law records, and domain databases.

  • Word mark & phonetic similarity analysis
  • Device/logo conflict check
  • Classification recommendation (all 45 classes)
  • Detailed written report shared via WhatsApp/email
02
Day 2 - Within 48 hrs

Application Drafting & Filing

Once you approve, our IP attorney drafts the application with correct classifications and descriptions. You digitally sign the authorization, and we file with the Trademark Registry the same day.

  • Power of Attorney (TM-48) prepared
  • Application form TM-A filed with govt.
  • Official filing receipt + application number shared
  • You can now use the ™ symbol
03
Month 3-6 - Examination

Examination & Journal Publication

The Registrar examines your application and may issue an examination report. We respond on your behalf within 30 days. If accepted, the mark is published in the Trademark Journal for 4 months.

  • Examination report reply (if raised)
  • Show-cause hearing representation
  • Journal publication monitoring
  • Opposition defence if filed by third party
04
Month 12-18 - Registration

Registration Certificate ®

If no opposition is received, the Registry issues your Registration Certificate. Your trademark is now officially protected for 10 years, renewable indefinitely.

  • Registration Certificate delivered
  • Use of ® symbol enabled
  • Added to our free renewal reminder system
  • Optional monitoring plan activated
